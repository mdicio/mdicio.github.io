Privacy policy

Franziska is a personal assistant used only by its owner, with their own Google account.

  • What it accesses: the owner's Google Calendar events (read and write) and Gmail messages (read only).
  • How the data is used: only to show the owner their schedule, remind them of tasks and summarize their own email. It is processed on the owner's own computers.
  • Sharing: the data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or model training.
  • Deletion: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
